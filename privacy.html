Privacy

Last updated July 31, 2026

This site is Lily Cummings' journalism portfolio. It collects very little, and this page describes all of it.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to see which pages get read and roughly where visitors come from. It sets cookies that start with _ga. It never shows us your name or exact location. You can block it in your browser or with Google's opt-out tool at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

The contact form

What you type in the contact form (name, email, subject, message) is delivered to Lily by Formspree, a form delivery service, and is used only to reply to you.

Fonts and video

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser requests those files from Google. News reels are embedded from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode, which sets no cookies until you press play.

Nothing else

No accounts, no ad tracking, no data sales. Some browsers send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals; the site does not change what it collects when it sees them, because this page already describes everything it collects.

Questions

Use the contact form and we will tell you what we have about you, correct it, or delete it.
